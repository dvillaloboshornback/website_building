Biography

I’m a senior at UC San Diego graduating in June 2026 with a B.S. in Business Economics and a Minor in Cognitive Science with a focus on Data Analysis. Getting here wasn’t linear either. I transferred, worked through COVID, and drove for Amazon after housing insecurity forced me to leave UC Santa Cruz. Those experiences shaped my resilience and passion for supporting others in higher education, which is why I currently work as a Retention Coordinator at UCSD helping students stay in school.

Academically, I’ve always been fascinated by the intersection of data, human behavior, and decision-making. Why and how people make the decisions they do. What started as a curiosity about Cognitive Science evolved into a love for data analysis. When I wrote my first working loop in Python, I was hooked; there’s something about writing code and getting it to work that hits like solving a puzzle. Since then I’ve applied that curiosity to real problems. My senior project investigated potential insider trading among U.S. politicians by analyzing their stock trades, committee memberships, and sector ROI. My long-term interest is in using data to understand how people and teams work, measuring productivity, diversity, and performance to help organizations build better, more effective teams.
